# MoneyPrinter — Automated Short-Form Video

**Status:** pipeline (script → TTS → stock footage → assembly) built and tested end to end. **Blocked** on identity-bound accounts.

## Checkpoints (Nicole, $0)

1. Get a free **Pexels API key** (pexels.com/api) under the LLC's business email.
2. Create or choose a **YouTube channel** owned by the LLC, then connect it for uploads.

An earlier plan used a **TikTok session cookie** for automated posting. That's dropped: reusing browser session cookies for automated uploads breaks TikTok's terms and risks a ban. Use TikTok's official Content Posting API, or post manually.

## Compliance

- Label AI-generated or synthetic content where the platform requires it (YouTube and TikTok both do).
- Use only properly licensed footage and music. Pexels content is free to use, and platform audio libraries are safe.
- Affiliate links need an FTC-compliant disclosure.
