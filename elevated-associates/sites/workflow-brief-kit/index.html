What's inside

  1. List candidate workflows. Five to eight repeatable tasks, with prompts to find them. (10 min)
  2. Score each one on frequency, time cost, clear steps, safe data, checkable output and low stakes, with rules for picking a safe first workflow. (15 min)
  3. Map how it works today: real steps, trigger, inputs, output, judgment calls and where it goes wrong. (15 min)
  4. Decide the boundary: what stays human, what AI drafts for review, and your data rules. (15 min)
  5. Write the one-page Workflow Brief: goal, process, split, data rules, success measure, acceptance criteria, risks and a first-30-days plan. (20 min)
